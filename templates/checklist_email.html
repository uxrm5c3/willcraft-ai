Dear {{ client_name or '[Client Name]' }},

Thank you for choosing us to assist with the preparation of your Last Will and Testament. To ensure a smooth and efficient process, please prepare the following documents and information before our meeting.

Key Documents Checklist

1. Personal Information

Your NRIC (MyKad) or Passport — clear photocopy of front and back.

2. Executor's Details

The person you wish to appoint to manage your estate. Please provide:

  • Full name (as per NRIC)
  • NRIC number
  • Current address
  • Relationship to you

You may appoint up to 2 joint executors and 1 substitute executor.

3. Beneficiary Information

Full names and NRIC/Passport numbers of all persons or organisations you wish to receive your assets. This includes:

  • Spouse, children, parents, siblings
  • Friends or other individuals
  • Charitable organisations (if any)
  • Substitute beneficiaries (who inherits if a beneficiary predeceases you)

4. Asset Details

Please prepare documents for the following assets:

Real Estate / Property

Land titles (Geran/Hakmilik/HSD), Sale & Purchase Agreements (SPA), or Cukai Harta statements. Include title number, lot number, and full address.

Financial Accounts

Bank passbooks, fixed deposit certificates, savings/current account statements. Note which accounts are sole or joint.

Investments

CDS (Central Depository Shares) statements, unit trust certificates (e.g., Public Mutual, ASNB), EPF statements.

Vehicles

Vehicle registration cards (Grant/Geran Kenderaan) with registration plate numbers.

Insurance / Takaful

Policy documents with policy numbers. Note: these often go by nomination, but listing them aids clarity.

Business Interests

Company registration certificates (SSM), partnership agreements, share certificates.

Personal Effects

A list of valuable personal items you wish to specifically bequeath (jewellery, art, collectibles, etc.).

5. Additional Documents

  • Marriage certificate (to verify spouse)
  • Birth certificates of children
  • Guardian details (if you have minor children — name, NRIC, address)
  • Any existing will or codicil (to be revoked)

6. Special Considerations

  • Any debts or liabilities on properties (mortgage/loan details)
  • Whether any property is jointly owned (and your share)
  • Special instructions (e.g., testamentary trust for minors, conditions on gifts)
  • Funeral or burial wishes

Important Notes

  • All names must match exactly as they appear on the NRIC/Passport.
  • You may bring photocopies — original documents are not required for drafting.
  • The will-signing appointment requires two independent witnesses (not beneficiaries) who are present at the same time.
  • If you do not understand English, please inform us so we can arrange a translator.

Should you have any questions or require further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.

We look forward to assisting you.
